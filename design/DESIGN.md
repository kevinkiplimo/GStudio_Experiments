## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an elite, nocturnal creative sanctum tailored for top-tier creators, broadcasters, and media moguls. It synthesizes the exclusivity of a private members' club with the precision and technical rigor of a state-of-the-art production soundstage. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an editorial dark mode layered with physical production metaphors: glowing "ON AIR" indicators, smoked-glass acoustic partitions, and crisp high-contrast typographic hierarchy. The design style fuses high-contrast minimalism with tactile broadcast glassmorphism. Surfaces feel dense, weighted, and acoustically insulated, illuminated by vibrant neon emissions and razor-sharp structural dividers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep stage environment where luminous signals command immediate attention:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#0A0A0C` (Absolute obsidian base)
  - Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Elevated Containers): `#121216`
  - Surface Tier 2 (Hover States, Flyouts, Sheets): `#1A1A22`
  - Borders & Dividers: `#23232A` (Low-contrast structural framing)
  - Hairline Highlight Borders: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

- **Accent Signals:**
  - Primary (`#FF3366`): "Broadcast Coral" representing live states, high-priority actions, and kinetic neon emission.
  - Secondary (`#FF9F1C`): "Tungsten Amber" evoking analog vu-meters, warm studio key-lighting, and premium status markers.
  - Tertiary (`#8A2BE2`): "Chroma Violet" providing deep secondary backlighting and ambient multi-camera isolation glows.

- **Content & Typography:**
  - Primary Content: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for high-impact editorial headlines)
  - Secondary Content: `#A1A1AA` (Muted slate for descriptive, readable body copy)
  - Muted/Disabled: `#52525B`

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances raw artistic authority with the technical exactitude of studio monitoring decks:

- **Display & Headlines (Syne):** Sculptural, wide-set letterforms convey bold creative confidence. Use tighter negative tracking on larger display sizes to maximize impact.
- **Body Text (Space Grotesk):** Provides a tech-forward, geometric cadence that remains exceptionally clear against dark surfaces.
- **Data, Badges & Technical Readouts (JetBrains Mono):** Applied exclusively to metadata, timecodes, live status tallies, hardware specs, and pricing metrics to invoke calibrated studio equipment. Always set in uppercase when used for status badges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional drop shadows, but on **luminous radiance, tonal layering, and frosted optical glass**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** `#0A0A0C` matte void.
- **Tier 1 (Acoustic Panels / Cards):** `#121216` backplane layered with a subtle hairline outline (`1px solid #23232A`). Hover transitions elevate the border to `rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.4)` accompanied by an ambient perimeter bloom.
- **Tier 2 (Glass Overlays & Viewfinder HUDs):** `rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.75)` backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and top edge rim-lighting (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Broadcast Glow System:** Interactive signals and live elements feature saturated optical glows:
  - *Live State Glow:* `0 0 24px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.35), 0 0 4px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.8)`
  - *Warm Tungsten Highlight:* `0 0 20px rgba(255, 159, 28, 0.25)`
  - *Chroma Accent Glow:* `0 0 32px rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.2)`

## Shapes

The system relies on an intentional dynamic contrast: **geometric pill silhouettes** for action triggers juxtaposed with **taut, subtly softened rectangles** for studio viewport framing.

- **Interactive Triggers (Pills):** Primary CTA buttons, status badges, and selector chips utilize full pill contours (`roundedness: 3`, resulting in 9999px border-radius) to feel tactile, sleek, and streamlined.
- **Media Displays & Structural Cards:** Framed with restrained radii (`0.75rem` to `1rem`), echoing high-end cinema field monitors and broadcast chassis.
- **Hardware Dials & Live Tally Indicators:** Strict circles (`50%` radius) featuring pulsating optical core indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Broadcast Pill:** Background `#FF3366`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk` medium. Smooth outer coral bloom on hover (`0 0 20px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.5)`). Active state applies a subtle scale compress (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Smoked Glass:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid #23232A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover changes border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` with internal rim glow.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text with JetBrains Mono tracking and an animated underline expansion in `#FF3366`.

### Badges & "ON AIR" Tallies
- **Live State Badge:** Fully rounded pill container, background `rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #FF3366`. Typography is `label-sm` in JetBrains Mono. Features a 6px circular dot pulsating via an ambient box-shadow keyframe.
- **Studio Spec Chip:** Dark fill `#1A1A22`, border `1px solid #23232A`, text `#A1A1AA`, label uppercase styling.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Studio Console Inputs:** Container background `#0E0E12`, border `1px solid #23232A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder in `#52525B`. Focus state shifts the border to `#FF3366` and introduces an ambient outer glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.25)`).
- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios):** Circular and squared monitors with custom inner neon cores that light up with `#FF3366` upon activation.

### Cards & Studio Bay Frames
- **Viewport Frame Card:** Background `#121216`, border `1px solid #23232A`, internal padding `space-lg`. Media containers inside cards utilize an anamorphic 16:9 or 2.39:1 crop with dark gradient overlays (`linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 40%, #121216 100%)`).
- **Telemetry Card:** JetBrains Mono headers, numerical stats set in large Syne typography, with an embedded linear VU-meter graphic.

### Lists & Audio Bus Panels
- Clean, border-bottom separated rows (`1px solid #23232A`). Hovering over a row highlights the background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` and highlights the lead index or timecode in `#FF9F1C`.
